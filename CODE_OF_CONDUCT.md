# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a technical community that is professional, constructive, and safe for contributors.

Examples of positive behavior include:

- discussing ideas on their technical merits;
- giving actionable review feedback;
- acknowledging uncertainty and correcting mistakes;
- respecting privacy and security boundaries;
- making room for contributors with different levels of experience.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, doxxing, threats, discriminatory conduct, deliberate disruption, or publishing sensitive security details before a fix is available.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, issues, code, and other contributions that violate this policy. Repeated or severe violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

Security reports should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not public discussion channels.
